# Quiet Weave identity

- Mechanism signature: complete time-band classification followed by deterministic longest-run selection.
- Product metaphor: a vertical loom whose coloured weft bands reveal a usable interval.
- Narrative voice: observant room host, short and tactile.
- Visual grammar: ultramarine frame, linen ground, vermilion shuttle, mint and yellow fibres.
- Interaction grammar: edit named spools, tension the minimum length, then pull one weaving handle.
- Result presentation: usable bands physically join into one bright strip across the cloth.
- Documentation style: textile workshop note rather than a protocol dossier.
- Evidence story: direct observations remain indexed in the sealed bundle and every band cites them.

Avoid clocks, circular layouts, forms as the first viewport, result tables, and source URL walls.
